## Lessons for future reviews

- Antd Modal has its own scroll container (.ant-modal-wrap); preserving window.scrollY does nothing for in-modal scroll. Avoid full-page re-renders while a modal is open; defer heavy reloads to modal close.
- useInfiniteScrollChunk resets visibleCount when the items array reference changes — any data refresh during scroll collapses the list and jumps to top. For bounded lists, render directly.
